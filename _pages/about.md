I am an adjunct instructor at ADA University in Baku, Azerbaijan, and a researcher working on machine learning for health and biomedical applications. My research focuses on generative models for medical imaging, in particular how we can tell whether synthetic images are clinically meaningful rather than just visually convincing.

## Research interests

- Machine learning for health and biomedical applications
- Generative modeling and synthetic medical data
- Computer vision and medical imaging
- Multimodal learning and human sensing
- Trustworthy and clinically meaningful AI

## Current research

**Synthetic mammogram generation.** I developed a lightweight Wasserstein GAN with gradient penalty (WGAN-GP) for generating synthetic mammograms, designed to train stably on consumer-grade hardware. Because image-quality metrics alone did not establish clinical plausibility, I built a validation framework that combines FID, SSIM, texture (GLCM) and edge analysis, GPT-4V assessment, and blinded physician evaluation. *(Publication: [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11219756))*

**Synthetic breast imaging review.** I am leading a scoping review and critical evidence audit of synthetic breast imaging, covering deep generative models and physics-based simulation across mammography, digital breast tomosynthesis, ultrasound and MRI. The review asks whether claims about synthetic data, especially for rare and underrepresented findings, are backed by appropriate fidelity, downstream-utility and clinical evidence. *(Manuscript in preparation.)*

**Breast cancer detection and labeling.** Earlier, I worked with surgeons and radiologists at Azerbaijan Medical University on a clinically informed breast cancer detection and labeling system, combining InceptionV3 deep features with GLCM texture features and comparing several classical classifiers. *(Publication: [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10325939))*
